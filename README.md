FandomVerse 🌌
Find Your Fandom. Find Your People.

FandomVerse is a fandom discovery and social networking platform concept
designed to help people explore pop-culture universes, discover new
interests, connect with like-minded fans, and build communities around
shared passions.

The project focuses on three core experiences:
- Discover fandoms across anime, K-pop, movies, TV shows, gaming,
  books, comics, and more.
- Find friends who share similar interests and fandoms.
- Connect with communities through discussions, posts, and
  messaging.
Project status: UI/UX design concept and interactive prototype.
Features requiring a backend are represented as prototype interactions
unless implemented separately.

✨ Features
🔎 Explore Fandoms
- Search fandoms by name, category, or interest.
- Browse Anime, K-pop, Movies, TV Shows, Gaming, Books, Comics, and
  other categories.
- Filter and sort fandoms by popularity, activity, and trending
  status.
- View fandom details, community activity, and related
  recommendations.
- Discover something new with the Surprise Me feature.
🫂 Find Friends
- Discover people with similar interests.
- Explore recommendations based on shared fandoms.
- View profiles, follow users, or send connection requests.
- Find potential gaming partners and fans of specific categories.
- See shared-interest indicators explaining why someone is
  recommended.
💬 Messages
- Browse conversations in a dedicated inbox.
- View message previews, timestamps, and unread indicators.
- Open conversations and simulate sending messages in the prototype.
- Explore message requests and conversation states.
- Access profile shortcuts and conversation privacy controls.
🏠 Community Hub
- Browse discussions, fan theories, polls, recommendations, and fan
  art.
- Like, comment on, save, and share posts.
- Explore community announcements and member activity.
- Discover communities related to favorite fandoms.
🎯 Personalized Discovery
- Choose favorite categories and interests during onboarding.
- Receive fandom and friend recommendations.
- Explore Recommended for You, Trending This Week, and
  Because You Like....
- View explanations for recommendations where available.
👤 Profiles, Notifications & Settings
- View profiles, favorite fandoms, joined communities, and personal
  collections.
- Receive notifications for messages, replies, followers, and
  community activity.
- Customize privacy, notification, and messaging preferences.
- Access blocking and reporting controls.

The design should include desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts, with

🎨 Design Direction
FandomVerse uses a dark, fandom-inspired visual identity.
- Backgrounds: Midnight purple, charcoal, and near-black
- Accent colors: Electric purple, violet, pink, and cyan
- Visual style: Rounded cards, subtle gradients, soft glows,
  expressive typography, and high-quality fandom imagery
- UX priorities: Clear navigation, accessible contrast, responsive
  layouts, meaningful feedback, and consistent components

🧪 Prototype Interactions
The interactive prototype is intended to demonstrate:
- Navigating between the main screens.
- Searching, filtering, and opening fandoms.
- Joining or following a fandom.
- Completing onboarding and viewing recommendations.
- Filtering friend suggestions and opening profiles.
- Opening a conversation and simulating message sending.
- Liking, commenting on, and saving community posts.
- Viewing notifications and editing profile settings.
- Displaying confirmation, empty, loading, and no-results states.
Note: Figma prototype interactions simulate product behavior. Real
authentication, persistent messaging, live presence, recommendations,
uploads, and database operations require a separately implemented
backend.

♿ UX, Safety & Privacy Principles
- Maintain readable typography and sufficient color contrast.
- Provide clear feedback for user actions.
- Make optional profile information genuinely optional.
- Give users control over who can message them.
- Include block and report options.
- Avoid exposing private information in public profiles.
- Provide useful empty states and accessible touch targets.
- Keep the experience welcoming to both new and experienced fans.
