# 🌌 FandomVerse

**Find Your Fandom. Find Your People.**

FandomVerse is a fandom discovery and social networking platform where users explore their favorite universes, discover new interests, find like-minded friends, and connect through communities and messaging.

## ✨ Features
- 🔎 **Explore Fandoms** — Discover anime, K-pop, movies, gaming, books, and more.
- 🫂 **Find Friends** — Connect with people who share your interests.
- 💬 **Messages** — Chat and stay connected.
- 🏠 **Communities** — Share fan art, discussions, polls, and theories.
- 🎯 **Personalized Discovery** — Get recommendations based on your interests.
- 👤 **Profiles & Settings** — Manage your fandoms, notifications, and privacy.

## 🎨 Design
A modern, responsive UI/UX featuring midnight purple backgrounds, vibrant pink and cyan accents, rounded cards, and immersive visuals.

## 🛠️ Suggested Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** React + TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Supabase
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Design & Prototype:** Figma

## 🧪 Prototype
The design includes 10 core screens, reusable components, responsive layouts, and interactive user flows. Backend-dependent features require separate implementation.

## 🚀 Future Plans
- Real-time messaging and authentication
- Personalized recommendations
- Fan-art sharing and community moderation
- Usability testing and responsive improvements
